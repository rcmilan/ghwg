# Ghost Hotel — vertical slice

Run from this directory:

```powershell
go run .
```

The executable's icon, a Shadow Person's black head, is `icon.ico`, linked in through `rsrc_windows_amd64.syso`; after changing the icon, rebuild that with `windres --preprocessor=cat -O coff -F pe-x86-64 icon.rc -o rsrc_windows_amd64.syso`.

Controls: `WASD` moves the concierge; `Space` opens the reception terminal (behind or in front of the counter), where `WASD` picks a room and `Space` calls a priest for it, and at the mirror on the lobby wall shifts the whole hotel between the real and the ghost dimension (through one of nine random 0.8 s transitions), at a booked room's door serves it in the real dimension; `Esc` closes the terminal, returns to title, or quits from it. Hold `T` to run the clock ×10 (dev aid).

How it plays: the hotel runs on a real-time clock, one in-game day in about 4 minutes, starting on Day 1 at 08:00. The hotel has two rooms side by side. Guests arrive already booked through an app, from the street during the day (none between 02:00 and 06:00, most from 14:00 to 22:00), and walk straight to their room; one comes only while a room it fits is empty. A party is one guest or a pair; a pair walks together, the second one a step behind, and needs a twin or double room. Rooms are Standard or Deluxe, with a single bed ($25 / $40 a night) or twin or double beds ($40 / $64), drawn when the run starts. The terminal pauses the world and shows each room's status, the money and the clock; there you pick a room and call a priest for it. Each guest stays 1–3 nights and is a homebody, who stays in, or a tourist, who goes out each morning (08:00–10:00) and comes back at night (19:00–22:00); the room stays booked while they're out, and the concierge can never enter a booked room. Once the whole party is inside with the door shut, the room fades into darkness; it clears only while its door is open, and stays dark while a tourist is out. The light follows the clock: clear by day, warm at dusk, deep blue at night under the corridors' ceiling lights, and pink at dawn. The lobby stays lit except from 01:00 to 06:00, and an empty room lights up while the concierge is in it. Your money shows on the terminal. On the last morning, between 10:00 and 12:00, the guest checks out: the whole stay is paid as it steps out of its door (`+$X`, rate × nights, up to 75% more for comfort) and the room is free again. At 23:00 guests walk into their beds, where only their heads show above the blanket, and get up at 07:00; the concierge can lie in an empty room's bed too. Awake in their room, guests keep busy: they look out of the windows, watch TV, browse the bookshelf, open the wardrobe or drawers, check the mirror or sit on the cushion, rest on their bed, stand at its foot glancing around, and a pair now and then stops to chat face to face. Guests in their room ask for room service once or twice a day while awake (07:00–23:00): a white `!!` balloon appears by their head, always on screen, pinned to the nearest edge when they're out of view. Serve them in the real dimension (SPACE at their door): their comfort rises, and with it what they pay. The sooner you get there, the more comfort, and the lower the room's Mood, the less (none once it's closed); after an hour unanswered the request expires and costs them comfort. Serving doesn't calm fear. In the ghost dimension the hotel turns arcade-colored, you see through the darkness into booked rooms. Fear (a red glow) comes only from a Shadow Person's sightings, and each point of it earns a point of Ectoplasm (shown in green and purple, and on the terminal); cleaning dirt in the ghost dimension earns it too. A guest frightened enough flees with the whole party, unpaid, and the room is free at once: they jump with an `AAAH!` and run out at triple speed. Every guest has a look of its own: hair, skin and clothes are recolored at random. Guests talk in speech balloons: `Hello!` on arrival, a greeting by the time of day when going out or coming back (then a pair chats between themselves), and now and then a line in their room: less cozy the lower the room's Mood. You hear guests only near the concierge, and in the real dimension not through walls; a guest asking for service says nothing else. Each room also has a Mood, from -100 (dread) to 100 (cozy): guests leave mess that lowers it. In the ghost dimension the whole room shifts toward green and purple as it falls (in the real one it only tints faintly green), and at -100 it pulses: the room is closed and no new guest comes to it. A priest called at the terminal (Father K, $40, halves the dread; Father M, $100, clears it) walks in, works half an hour and lifts it. The terminal no longer sells a Shadow Person (the Hex is gone too); a Shadow (dev: `-shadow`) is a black figure that dashes along a room's back wall while guests are in, more often the lower the room's Mood. Each run frightens a guest who sees it (it hops and cries out) more than one who doesn't, and the shadow keeps the room uneasy, but it never scares anyone into fleeing. Father M banishes it.

The supplied Modern Interiors source pack remains untouched at the workspace root as requested. The `assets` package is the replacement boundary for future LimeZu sprite-sheet slicing; the current clean geometric presentation keeps the project runnable until those scattered files are curated. The W95FA font source is not present locally, so Ebitengine's built-in debug glyphs are used temporarily.

## Dev tools

Simulation mode plays many Runs headless and in parallel, each by a Strategy that uses only the keys a player has, and prints a score per day (money plus Ectoplasm at `-ectorate`, default $0.5 a point: an assumed shop price), money per day, occupancy, Bookings, parties fled, Ectoplasm, serves, expired requests, priests called, their cost, room-hours closed and invariant failures per Strategy. `never` stands at the terminal; `server` stays real and serves every room that asks; `keeper` also keeps the supplies stocked and cleans empty rooms' dirt in whichever dimension it's in; `smart` serves, cleans empty rooms in the ghost dimension and calls a weak priest to a closed room; `shopper` buys every unlock and priest on sale as soon as it can afford it, and plays `smart` in between:

```powershell
go run ./cmd/sim -strategy never,server,keeper,smart,shopper -runs 64 -days 7 -grid 2x1 -seed 1 -react 30 -keygap 6 -csv out.csv
```

`-react` and `-keygap` set the Pilot's pace in frames (`0` `0` is frame-perfect), `-nocheck` skips the invariants, `-csv` writes one row per Run. Run `i` uses seed `seed+i` for every Strategy. Any row replays on screen with `go run . -play smart -seed 7` (watch mode).

Prints: `go run . -shot out.png -at 2400` starts a Run, fast-forwards `-at` frames (600 a game hour) and saves one screen; `-ghost` starts it in the ghost dimension.
