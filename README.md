# WEIRD GUESTS

You run a hotel that exists twice. In the real world, living guests pay for their rooms. In the ghost world, the dead check into the same rooms and never leave. You are the Concierge, the only one who walks both sides.

Made in a month for [GitHub Game Off 2026](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2026). The theme was **SHIFT**.

**Play it in your browser:** *(itch.io link coming soon)*

## How to play

Serve the living, keep the dead happy, and grow the hotel.

- Guests ask for towels, food and the like. Fetch them from the kitchen and take them to the door.
- Step through the lobby Mirror to SHIFT into the ghost world, where the hotel turns strange colours and the ghosts show up.
- A frightened guest pays in Ectoplasm. A terrified one runs off without paying. Find the line.
- Spend money and Ectoplasm at the reception Terminal: new rooms, staff, upgrades, a priest or two.
- Somewhere along the way, a witch checks in. She has errands.

## Controls

The game needs a keyboard. Click the game once so it can hear your keys.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Walk |
| `Space` | Do the thing in front of you (whatever glows) |
| `Esc` | Close what's open, or pause |
| `T` (hold) | Run time 4x faster |

The game saves itself every morning (from Day 2). Come back later and pick CONTINUE.
